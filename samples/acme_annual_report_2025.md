# Acme Retail — Annual Report 2025

*Fictional sample document for the SLM RAG Lab. All names and figures are invented.*

## 1. Summary

Acme Retail had its strongest year since 2019. Revenue grew 12 percent to 4.2 billion dollars, driven mainly by online sales, which now make up 38 percent of total revenue (up from 31 percent in 2024). Operating margin improved from 18 to 21 percent after the company closed 40 underperforming stores. Customer satisfaction, measured by Net Promoter Score (NPS), rose from 31 to 44.

## 2. Financial results

| Metric | 2024 | 2025 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 3.75 billion dollars | 4.2 billion dollars | +12% |
| Online share of revenue | 31% | 38% | +7 pts |
| Operating margin | 18% | 21% | +3 pts |
| Logistics costs | 110 million dollars | 120 million dollars | +9% |
| Number of stores | 410 | 370 | −40 |
| Employees | 28,500 | 27,900 | −2% |

The gross margin on online orders (34 percent) remains lower than in stores (41 percent), mainly because of delivery costs and a higher return rate (14 percent online vs 6 percent in stores).

## 3. Customers

Active customers grew to 11.2 million. The loyalty programme, launched in 2023, now has 4.1 million members, who spend on average 2.3 times more per year than non-members. Same-day delivery, available in 15 cities, was used for 18 percent of online orders in those cities. The most common complaints were late deliveries (31 percent of complaints), damaged items (17 percent) and difficulty reaching customer support (15 percent).

## 4. Risks

The board identified three main risks for 2026:

1. **Rising logistics costs.** Logistics costs grew 9 percent in 2025, faster than revenue in the second half of the year. Fuel prices and driver wages are the main drivers. If costs keep rising at this rate, the operating margin could fall by about 1.5 points by 2027.
2. **Customer support capacity.** Support contact volume grew 22 percent while the support team grew 5 percent. Average wait time rose from 4 to 11 minutes.
3. **Competition in same-day delivery.** Two national competitors launched same-day delivery in Acme's largest markets in 2025.

## 5. Recommendations for 2026

The board recommends three priorities:

1. **Expand same-day delivery to 25 more cities**, focusing on cities where Acme already has a store within 20 km, to use stores as local fulfilment hubs and limit logistics costs.
2. **Invest 300 million dollars in a new loyalty programme** with personalised offers and free returns for members, targeting 6 million members by the end of 2026.
3. **Pilot AI-assisted customer support in two regions** before any wider rollout. The pilot will be judged on wait time, resolution rate and customer satisfaction. Answers about refunds and account changes must be reviewed by a human agent during the pilot.

The board does not recommend opening new stores in 2026.

## 6. Leadership

The CEO, Maria Lopez, who has led the company since 2018, will step down in June 2026. The board has started a search for her successor and expects to announce an appointment in the first quarter of 2026. CFO David Chen will remain in his role.

## 7. Sustainability

Acme cut emissions from its own operations by 11 percent in 2025, mainly by switching 120 stores to renewable electricity. Electric vans now make up 22 percent of the delivery fleet; the target is 50 percent by 2028.
